# Startup Investment Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses SQL to analyze startup investment data from Crunchbase. The goal is to explore funding patterns, compare startup outcomes, and investigate trends within the clean technology (cleantech) industry.

## Objectives

- Identify the most heavily funded startups.
- Examine highly funded companies that have closed.
- Investigate funding and closure patterns among cleantech companies.
- Use SQL filtering, sorting, pattern matching, and aggregate analysis to answer business questions.

## Tools & Skills

- SQL
- Data filtering with `WHERE`
- Sorting and ranking with `ORDER BY`
- Handling missing values with `IS NOT NULL`
- Pattern matching with `ILIKE` and wildcards
- Business and industry trend analysis

## Key Findings

- **Top-funded startup:** Clearwire appeared as the highest-funded company in the query results.
- **Closed startups:** Abound Solar was the highest-funded closed company identified, with $510 million in reported funding.
- **Cleantech representation:** Six of the twelve highest-funded closed companies were categorized as cleantech.
- **Cleantech dataset:** 898 companies were categorized as cleantech in the dataset.
- **Company name analysis:** 275 cleantech companies had “solar,” “power,” or “energy” in their names.

## Business Insights

The results suggest that substantial startup funding does not guarantee long-term business success. Cleantech companies may face challenges such as high development costs, regulatory requirements, infrastructure needs, and dependence on market conditions.

These findings highlight the importance of evaluating both funding levels and company outcomes when assessing investment opportunities.

## Limitations

This analysis uses the dataset and company status information available for the assignment. The results describe patterns in this dataset and do not establish that funding causes a company to succeed or fail. Company status and funding information may also change over time.
